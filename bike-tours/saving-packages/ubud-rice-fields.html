Let's try Ubud Rice Fields (Saving Package)

Lunch⛔, pick up and drop off hotel⛔

Explore peaceful village roads and lush rice field trails in the Ubud countryside (about 30 minutes from Central Ubud). This 12 km route combines flat paths with gentle rolling hills for light, refreshing exercise. Lunch and hotel transfers are not included in this Saving Package.

1. Come to Start point :

You can come to our family basecamp using the transportation you have, such as a Taxi, Gojek, or Grab.

Recommended Meeting Time at Start Point: Morning session at 09:00 AM (Ubud) / 09:45 AM (Kintamani), or Afternoon session at 02:00 PM (flexible for Private Groups).

2. Start Cycling :

Upon arriving at our family basecamp, hop onto your bicycle and embark on your normal bike journey through Bali's lush countryside. Located about 30 minutes from Central Ubud, this 12 km route combines flat village roads, gentle rolling hills, and scenic rice fields for light, refreshing exercise. An E-Bike option is also available for this route.

3. Visits to Unique Locations :

Along the route, you'll explore a variety of unique locations, including :

  • ▪ Local School : Gain insights into Bali's education system.
  • ▪ Family Compound : Be a welcomed guest in a traditional Balinese family compound and interact with local residents.
  • ▪ Village Temple : Delve into the spiritual practices and cultural significance of a traditional Balinese temple.
  • ▪ Rice Fields : We will cycle from village to village, with 80% of the route surrounded by rice field views.

#Some locations may be omitted in case of overtime, as we prioritize your enjoyment and want to ensure you don't get bored.

4. Cultural Immersion :

Throughout the tour, engage with locals, ask questions, and learn about Balinese culture, enriching your overall experience.

*Note :
  • ▪ Our suggestion is to start at 6:30 - 07:00 am, because traffic is lighter in the morning, and the air is fresher. Or start in the afternoon at 3:00 pm. However, the tour is flexible, and we can start at any time.ⓘ Time request (custom start time) is available for Private Group only.
  • ▪ The duration is 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on how you ride the bike.

Ubud Cycling FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Ubud Cycling Tour

Everything you need to know about our private Ubud rice field bike ride.

Our tour is 100% private—you ride exclusively with your own family, partner, or friends without strangers. This allows you to cycle at your preferred pace, pause anytime for photos, and enjoy authentic cultural explanations from our friendly native Balinese guides.

The cycling distance is approximately 12 kilometers with a duration of 2 to 2.5 hours. The terrain consists of tranquil rural asphalt roads, gentle undulations, and paved Subak rice field paths. It is 80% surrounded by scenic greenery and is suitable for all fitness levels.
