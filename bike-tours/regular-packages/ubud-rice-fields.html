Ubud Rice Fields Cycling with Meal and Pool (Regular Package)

Sizing & Bikes:

  • • Childseat age 1 - 4. Adults bike size for M - XL
  • • Backseat age 5 - 7. Size bike only for M
  • • Small bike for min height 120cm, lower than 120cm can use a backseat
  • • Small bike for min height 135cm
  • • Adults bike, size from S - XL
1. Hotel Pickup :

Begin your day with a convenient pickup from your hotel.

2. Drive to Start point :

The starting point is at my house or nearby, where you have the opportunity to test the bikes before beginning your cycling journey.

3. Start Cycling :

Upon arriving my house, hop onto your bicycle and embark on your normal bike journey, all while taking in the natural views of Bali's landscapes. This trip is combination of uphill, downhill, flat, small road, rice fields. Go to start point 15 - 20 minutes from Ubud. The uphill and downhill are not steep. We will ride for 12 km, this trip is more exercise. We have e-bike option for this trip.

4. Visits to Unique Locations :

Along the route, you'll explore a variety of unique locations, including :

  • ▪ Local School : Gain insights into Bali's education system.
  • ▪ Family Compound : Be the guest in Family Compound and interact with residents.
  • ▪ Village Temple : Delve into the spiritual practices and cultural significance of a traditional Balinese temple.
  • ▪ Rice Fields : We will cycle from village to village, with 80% of the route is surrounded by rice field views.

#Some locations may be omitted in case of overtime, as we prioritize your enjoyment and want to ensure you don't get bored.

5. Cultural Immersion :

Throughout the tour, engage with locals, ask questions, and learn about Balinese culture, enriching your overall experience.

6. Indonesian Lunch :

Conclude your tour by savoring a delightful Indonesian meal and indulging in the inviting swimming pool at the restaurant.

7. Return Transfers :

We ensure convenient and safe return transfers.

*Note :
  • ▪ Our suggestion is to start at 6:30 - 07:00 am, because traffic is lighter in the morning, and the air is fresher. Or start in the afternoon at 3:00 pm. However, the tour is flexible, and we can start at any time.
  • ▪ The duration is 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on how you ride the bike.
