Tour Program:

Hotel Pickup : Begin your day with a convenient pickup from your hotel.

Drive to Kintamani : Our journey starts with a scenic drive to the breathtaking natural views of the Kintamani volcano area. We call it Batur Volcano.

Start Cycling : Upon arriving in Kintamani, hop onto your bicycle and the route would be 80% downhill from village to village, all while taking in the natural views of Bali's landscapes. Riding from Kintamani until Ubud, the road is not steep down.

Visits to Unique Locations :

Along the route, you'll explore a variety of unique locations, including :

  • Local School : Gain insights into Bali's education system.
  • Family Compound : Be the guest in Family Compound and interact with residents.
  • Village Temple : Delve into the spiritual practices and cultural significance of a traditional Balinese temple.
  • Rice Fields : Immerse yourself in the lush greenery of Balinese rice fields and understand the island's agricultural system.

Cultural Immersion : Throughout the tour, engage with locals, ask questions, and learn about Balinese culture, enriching your overall experience.

Indonesian Lunch : Conclude your tour by savoring a delightful Indonesian meal.

Return Transfers : We ensure convenient and safe return transfers.

*Note: If you can't ride a bike, sit in the car, because the car will follow all the time. The duration is 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on how you ride the bike.

ITINERARY

Morning Trip: • Pick up at 8:30 AM
• Go to Kintamani volcano area
• After that go to start point
• Start cycling around 10 AM
• Cycling through villages and rice fields (stop at a Balinese house, refreshment fruit from garden)
• Finish around 12:30 PM & Lunch (Balinese food)
• Return to hotel around 1:30 PM
Afternoon Trip: • Pick up at 11:45 AM
• Go to lunch first or later finish
• Go to Kintamani volcano area
• Start cycling around 2 PM
• Cycling through villages and rice fields
• Stop at Balinese house & fruit refreshment
• Finish around 5 PM and back to hotel
