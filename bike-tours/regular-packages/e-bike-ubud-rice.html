⚡ Why Choose Our Ubud E-Bike Tour?

Unlike standard Ubud cycling tours that require extra leg effort on rolling village hills, our electric pedal-assist E-Bike tour gives you smooth motor support whenever you pedal—letting you enjoy the 12 km countryside route without breaking a sweat.

  • Effortless on Uphills: Pedal-assist motor automatically helps on gentle slopes and village inclines.
  • Beginner & Senior Friendly: Ideal for casual riders, couples, and travelers who want scenic sightseeing without exhaustion.
  • Full Battery & Test Ride: Fully charged electric bikes with a safety briefing and practice ride before starting.
1. Hotel Pickup :

Begin your day with a convenient pickup from your hotel.

2. Drive to Start point :

The starting point is at our family basecamp or nearby, where you have the opportunity to test the bikes before beginning your cycling journey.

3. Start Cycling :

Upon arriving at our family basecamp, hop onto your bicycle and embark on your electric bike journey through Bali's lush countryside. Located about 30 minutes from Central Ubud, this 12 km route combines flat village roads, mild rolling hills, and scenic rice fields with effortless pedal-assist support.

4. Visits to Unique Locations :

Along the route, you'll explore a variety of unique locations, including :

  • ▪ Local School : Gain insights into Bali's education system.
  • ▪ Family Compound : Be a welcomed guest in a traditional Balinese family compound and interact with local residents.
  • ▪ Village Temple : Delve into the spiritual practices and cultural significance of a traditional Balinese temple.
  • ▪ Rice Fields : We will cycle from village to village, with 80% of the route surrounded by rice field views.

#Some locations may be omitted in case of overtime, as we prioritize your enjoyment and want to ensure you don't get bored.

5. Cultural Immersion :

Throughout the tour, engage with locals, ask questions, and learn about Balinese culture, enriching your overall experience.

6. Indonesian Lunch :

Conclude your tour by savoring a delightful Indonesian meal and indulging in the inviting swimming pool at the restaurant.

7. Return Transfers :

We ensure convenient and safe return transfers.

*Note :
  • ▪ Minimum height: 135 cm (4'5").
  • ▪ Our suggestion is to start at 6:30 - 07:00 am, because traffic is lighter in the morning, and the air is fresher. Or start in the afternoon at 3:00 pm. However, the tour is flexible, and we can start at any time.ⓘ Time request (custom start time) is available for Private Group only.
  • ▪ The duration is 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on how you ride the bike.
