Fasilitas & Standar Keamanan Kami

Keselamatan dan kenyamanan Anda adalah prioritas utama. Seluruh armada sepeda dan kendaraan dirawat berkala oleh tim teknisi kami.

EQUIPMENT & FLEET

Peralatan Berkualitas untuk Kenyamanan Maksimal

TRANSPORTATION

Car to carry bike and pick up guest

Car pick up guests: Air-conditioned clean vehicle for guest pick-up and drop-off to your hotel.

Car carrying bicycle: Specially equipped support truck carrying bicycles that follows all the time during the tour.

GEAR & BIKES

Bike and helmet

Normal Bike: Mountain bikes with suspension and Shimano gears (Size S - XL, and small bike for min height 120cm & 135cm).

E-bike: Electric bikes with pedal assist (size S - M, min height 150cm).

Helmet: Clean sanitized helmets with adjustable straps for all sizes.

MAINTENANCE

Bike Mechanic

The Mechanic (Dewa Munduk): Professional bike mechanic with 14+ years experience ensuring all bikes are safe and tuned up before every ride.

Restaurant: End your tour with delicious Balinese lunch and relaxing swimming pool access.
