ABOUT US

Let's Explore Bali Island with DEWA BIKE

Hello I am Dewa Munduk

I started working on a cycling tour in 2010 when I was still working as a bicycle carrier driver for a cycling tour company. In 2011, I tried to buy a bicycle and started a bicycle rental business and organized cycling tours under the name Sewa Sepeda Bali, I get a lot of customers from agents and companies who do outbound while cycling.

In 2022, I started to establish the Dewa Bike, which focuses on cycling trips with private bike tours in Bali. We want to provide tourists with a fun and eco-friendly cycling experience, as well as raise awareness of sustainable tourism. Ends with us to enjoy the charm of Bali in a different way and appreciate its natural beauty.

— Dewa Munduk

Enjoy your holiday in Bali with DEWA BIKE

Welcome to Bali Island. Hello, my name is Dewa Eka, son of Dewa Munduk, the founder of Dewa Bike. Together, the two of us created this company from scratch, from creating a website, taking photos and videos, to marketing activities. I hope that the Dewa Bike can be a means of introducing Balinese culture to foreign tourists globally, as well as introducing private bike tours in Bali.

— Dewa Eka (Dode)

Dewa Bike Tour

Dewa Bike is a small family business in Bali since 09-04-2023, owned and operated by the Dewa Family who are committed to providing an unforgettable cycling experience on the beautiful island of Bali. We offer a variety of bike tour packages that can be tailored to your preferences and comfort level. With Dewa Bike, you will experience the freedom and joy of cycling in Bali without having to worry about the details of the trip, because we will take care of everything for you.
