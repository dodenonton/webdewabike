Ubud River Tubing Adventure (Lunch Included)

Rp. 250.000 / person for all ages • Indonesian Lunch Included ✅

Experience fun on the water with our dedicated River Tubing tour! Float and glide down peaceful canyon streams surrounded by lush tropical riverbanks, dramatic stone canyons, and tranquil rainforest views. Perfect for families, children, and friends of all ages looking for a refreshing water adventure in Bali.

🤝

Partner Story & Special Discounted Rate

As an independent partner, the standard rate for this package is IDR 350,000 per person; however, I am able to offer a special discounted rate through my membership status. I originally began solely as a cycling tour provider before collaborating with Gekko Tubing to design our signature cycling and tubing combo packages. Through this partnership, I earned a special membership tier, with a reward awaiting once my referral target is met. Thank you to everyone who has booked via our website—your support means a lot and helps me achieve this milestone.

About the Tubing Operation & Camera Notice :
  • ▪ All-Inclusive Pricing: Flat rate of Rp. 250.000 per person for all ages (adults and children).
  • ▪ Lunch Included: A freshly prepared, authentic Indonesian meal is served at the restaurant after your river trip.
  • ▪ Local Community River: The tubing operation is proudly managed by the local village community, with experienced native river guides ensuring full safety throughout your journey.
  • ▪ Camera & Photo Service: For safety while paddling, personal cameras are not permitted on the river 📸❌. The local village team provides an optional high-resolution photo & video service (IDR 300,000 for 1–4 people; +IDR 50,000/person for larger groups).

WHAT TO BRING:

Swimwear or comfortable water clothes, change of dry clothes, flip-flops or water shoes, sunscreen, towel, and cash for photo service or personal drinks.
