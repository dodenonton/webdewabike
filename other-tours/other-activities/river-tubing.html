River Tubing in Ubud Bali

Experience fun on the water with our tubing package! Glide down the river, perfect for families and friends. Enjoy the lush natural tropical river banks, pristine canyon scenery, and gentle flowing currents.

About the Tubing Operation:
The tubing operation is managed by the local village community, and only local residents are allowed to use the river for this business.

Camera & Photo Service Notice:
Guests aren't allowed to bring their own cameras📸❌. They do provide a photo and video service for IDR 300,000/one - four people. For groups larger than 4, it's an extra IDR 50,000 per person.

Combine with Bike Tour for the Best Value:

You can combine River Tubing with our signature Kintamani downhill cycling tour! Start with morning downhill cycling through villages and rice fields, enjoy Balinese lunch, and glide down the river in the afternoon.
