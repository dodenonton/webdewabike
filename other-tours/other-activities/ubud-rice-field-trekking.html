AUTHENTIC CULTURAL IMMERSION

Ubud: Balinese Village and Rice Fields Guided Trek

Immerse yourself in the authentic rhythm of daily Balinese life. This journey is carefully designed to blend warm cultural interactions with the soothing natural beauty of Ubud.

🌅 Morning Session (8:30 AM) • Start: 8:30 AM (Duration: 2 Hours)
• Cooler breeze & softer morning lighting
• Watch kindergarten children at school & early morning village life
🌇 Afternoon Session (2:30 PM) • Start: 2:30 PM (Duration: 2 Hours)
• Warm golden hour lighting across emerald rice terraces
• Peaceful afternoon walk before your evening dinner

🧭 Tour Itinerary & Highlights:

STOP 1

Morning Smiles at a Local Kindergarten

Begin your journey with positive energy by visiting a local village kindergarten. You will have the opportunity to witness the joy and innocence of the village children as they start their day of learning and playing, offering a pure and heartwarming glimpse into the lives of Bali's younger generation.

STOP 2

Spiritual Tranquility at the Village Temple

Your next step brings you to the Village Temple (Pura Desa), the spiritual heart and epicenter of the local community's religious activities. Soak in the peaceful atmosphere and admire the intricate details of traditional Balinese stone carvings, a sacred space where locals offer their daily prayers of gratitude.

STOP 3

A Warm Welcome at a Traditional Balinese Compound

Stop by a traditional Balinese family compound to experience genuine local hospitality. Welcomed as an honored guest, you will relax in a traditional pavilion (bale) with the host family. Enjoy this intimate moment while savoring authentic home treats—from rich Balinese coffee and warm tea to fresh traditional snacks (jajanan) prepared by the family that day.

STOP 4

Exploring the Village & Ubud's Rice Fields

After a heartwarming visit, embark on a leisurely walk through the charming village streets. The residential scenery will gracefully transition into the vast, lush green expanse of Ubud's rice fields. Take in the harmony of rural nature, the open skies, and the ancestral subak irrigation system still actively used by local farmers today.

STOP 5

Refreshing Break with a Fresh Young Coconut

Midway through your trek, take a well-deserved pause surrounded by beautiful natural landscapes. You will be treated to a freshly picked young coconut. Sipping the sweet coconut water straight from the shell while enjoying the gentle breeze of the rice fields is the perfect, natural thirst quencher.

STOP 6

Strolling Along the Paddies to the Finish Point

With your energy restored, continue your walk along the narrow footpaths winding through the rice paddies. This final stretch offers the most picturesque vantage points of Ubud’s agricultural landscape, guiding you peacefully to the finish point and concluding your tour with unforgettable cultural memories.
