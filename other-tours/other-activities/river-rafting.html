Join Our Rafting Adventure

Join our rafting adventure for an exciting journey down the river! Perfect for groups seeking thrills and teamwork. Paddle through exhilarating rapids, carved stone reliefs on river valley walls, and refreshing waterfalls along the lush Ayung river basin in Ubud.

Combination Tour with Cycling:

Cycling + Rafting (From Rp. 850.000/adult):
We can arrange the itinerary as follows: a morning cycling tour, followed by rafting in the afternoon. We can either have lunch in between or do the rafting first, then have lunch.

What's Included in Rafting:

  • ✔️ Professional certified river guide
  • ✔️ International standard rafting gear (helmets, life jackets, paddles)
  • ✔️ Waterproof bag for valuables
  • ✔️ Shower, towels, and clean changing rooms
  • ✔️ Insurance coverage
  • ✔️ Delicious buffet lunch
