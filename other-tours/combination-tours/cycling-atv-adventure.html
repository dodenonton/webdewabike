Cycling Tour + ATV Adventure

Kintamani Downhill Cycling:

On this tour, the roads are 80% of the route being downhill. Go to the start point around 45 minutes - 1 hour from Ubud. Before the start point, we will stop in Batur volcano view point, to see Batur volcano and lake.

ATV Adventure:

We can arrange the itinerary as follows: a morning cycling tour, followed by ATV in the afternoon. We can either have lunch in between or do the ATV first, then have lunch.

1. Hotel Pickup :

Begin your day with a convenient pickup from your hotel at 8.30 am.

2. Drive to Kintamani :

Our journey starts with a scenic drive to the breathtaking natural views of the Kintamani volcano area. We call it Batur Volcano.

3. Start Cycling :

Upon arriving in Kintamani, hop onto your bicycle and the route would be 80% downhill from village to village, all while taking in the natural views of Bali's landscapes. Riding from Kintamani until Ubud, the road is not steep down.

4. Visits to Unique Locations :

Along the route, you'll explore a variety of unique locations, including :

  • ▪ Local School : Gain insights into Bali's education system.
  • ▪ Family Compound : Be the guest in Family Compound and interact with residents.
  • ▪ Village Temple : Delve into the spiritual practices and cultural significance of a traditional Balinese temple.
  • ▪ Rice Fields : Immerse yourself in the lush greenery of Balinese rice fields and understand the island's agricultural system.
5. Cultural Immersion :

Throughout the tour, engage with locals, ask questions, and learn about Balinese culture, enriching your overall experience.

6. Indonesian Lunch :

Conclude your tour by savoring a delightful Indonesian meal.

7. ATV Adventure :

After cycling, we can continue with a ATV adventure.

8. Return Transfers :

We ensure convenient and safe return transfers.

*Note :
  • ▪ After cycling: lunch, or straight to ATV Adventure.
  • ▪ If you can't ride a bike, sit in the car, because the car will follow all the time.
  • ▪ The duration is 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on how you ride the bike.
