DEWA BIKE TOUR

Our Bike Tours are Awesome!

Dewa Bike offers the best way to experience an Ubud bicycle tour. We offer a variety of cycling tour packages that will ensure you thoroughly enjoy your vacation time in Bali.

We have 2 different trip:

⛰️
1. Kintamani Downhill Cycling (Normal Bike)

The road would be downhill, it is not much to peddle the bike, mostly breaking. Go to start point around 45 minutes - 1 hour from Ubud. Before come to start point, we will stop in Batur volcano view point, to see Batur volcano and lake. We will ride around 18km.

🌾
2. Trip in Ubud Countryside (Normal Bike & E-Bike)

Combination of uphill, downhill, flat, small road, rice fields. Go to start point 15 - 20 minutes from Ubud. The uphill and downhill are not steep. We will ride for 12 km, this trip is more exercise. For e-bike min height 150cm.

Normally, we will ride for 2 - 2,5 hours cycling included stop in some places like local primary school, a Balinese house, stop in front of local temple and cycling inside of rice field.
We also can do both trips together. The distance until 40 km.

TOUR ROUTE & TIMELINE

Sample Tour Itinerary: Kintamani Downhill (Regular)

A well-paced daily itinerary designed for complete comfort for you and your family. Timing may vary slightly depending on stops or local traffic.

07:30 - 08:30

Hotel Pickup in AC Vehicle

Our comfortable air-conditioned car picks you up directly from your hotel in Ubud, Sanur, Kuta, Seminyak, or Nusa Dua and heads up to the Kintamani highlands.

09:00 - 09:45

Visit Mount Batur Volcano Viewpoint

Visit the panoramic Mount Batur viewpoint to take in breathtaking views and capture photos overlooking the active volcano and Lake Batur.

10:00

Bike Fitting & Safety Briefing

Saddle height adjustment, helmet fitting, comprehensive brake and safety check, and a short group safety briefing.

10:15 - 12:30

Downhill Cycling Experience (18 km)

Gentle downhill cycling through the serene Pengotan bamboo forest, stopping at a traditional Balinese family compound, local primary school, and village temples.

12:45 - 13:45

Lunch with Authentic Indonesian Dishes

Arrive at the finish line, freshen up, and enjoy a delicious spread of authentic Indonesian dishes in a relaxed tropical garden setting.

14:00

Comfortable Return to Hotel

Our driver transfers you back to your hotel comfortably with wonderful memories from your Bali cycling adventure.

Regular Package (Rp 550,000) is fully all-inclusive: round-trip air-conditioned hotel transfers, authentic Indonesian buffet lunch, Mount Batur volcano viewpoint admission, and all cycling equipment. Meanwhile, Saving Package (Rp 350,000) is tailored for self-drive travelers who arrive directly at the meeting point with their own vehicle and wish to arrange their own lunch.

We always carry disposable rain ponchos in our support vehicle. In light drizzle, cycling through the green rice fields is actually a refreshingly cool and scenic experience. If weather conditions ever become extreme or unsafe, tours can be rescheduled free of charge without penalty.
